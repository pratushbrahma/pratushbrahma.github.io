CRITTER CUTOUTS (the animals that crawl across the home page)
=============================================================

When you click an animal on the home-page habitat map, it scuttles across
the screen and opens that research. By default it uses a little emoji. Drop a
realistic cutout here and it will use that instead, automatically.

Use transparent-background PNGs (the animal cut out, no square background),
named exactly:

    spider.png      (jumping spider)
    jewelfish.png   (African jewelfish)
    frog.png        (a frog)
    agama.png       (rock agama)
    termite.png     (termite)
    mouse.png       (mouse)

No file for an animal = it keeps using the emoji. Nothing breaks either way.

GOOD SIZE: roughly 200-400 px on the longest side, under ~150 KB each.

WHERE TO GET THEM (free, with credit):
  - iNaturalist (inaturalist.org): find a photo licensed CC-BY or CC0, then
    cut out the animal (remove.bg or any photo editor) and save as PNG.
  - PhyloPic (phylopic.org): clean silhouettes of organisms, many CC0. These
    look great as simple, naturalist shapes.
  - Wikimedia Commons: search the species, filter by reusable licenses.

Please keep a note of the photographer/author and license for anything that
is CC-BY, so you can credit them (a small credits line on the page is fine,
just tell me and I will add it).
